WEBE

WEBE was a hackathon project that resulted in a google extension made to prevent users from scrolling through short form content for long periods of time and before deadlines.

By clicking the extension a menu will appear allowing you to add tasks with their duration, difficulty, and deadline. This information will be used to determine how many scrolls per session are allowed.

When you scroll past too many videos, the extension shows a prompt with a AI messenger that tries to convince you to stop scrolling. After this limit is reached too many times the extension blocks the window you are scrolling on.

Trillium Flower Toy

This is a video of the Trillium Flower Toy being assembled, it has 17 components in total (10 unique components) and was made with a group of other students.

The goal of this project was to make a 3D printable puzzle that had some mechanism in it. This toy has a twistable base that pushes the pedals out creating a "blooming" effect.

This is a cutaway view showing the internal corkscrew mechanism. it uses a plunger inside two shells with their own guidrails. the inner shell has a srtaigth vertical guidrail and the outer shell has a helical guidrail. When a small pin is inserted itno these three components it creates a corkscrew mechanism that works similarily to a lipstick case.

Heres a close-up of the corkscrew mechanism. The slope of the helical track can determine how fast the plunger moves up as the base is twisted. We chose a relatively small slope to prevent jamming but had to ensure it wasnt too small so there would be enough material between the track to keep the whole structure ridgid.
